# ASUFC Finance presentation

## 1. Why ASUFC needed a ledger

- Several funding sources can support a single program.
- Staff need cash, credits, confirmed spending and pending funding to reconcile separately.
- Each source can have different restrictions and deadlines.

## 2. How staff use it

- Launch an installed app on the designated computer.
- Review funding and budgets, then enter or review a purchase.
- Select eligible allocations, attach a receipt and confirm the record.

## 3. Demonstrate a purchase

- Use fictional cash and purchasing credits.
- Split a purchase, review the updated balances and filter the spending report.
- Attach a receipt, replace it with a reason and show the original in history.

## 4. Demonstrate offline recovery

- Save an encrypted backup under Account.
- Restore it into a fresh profile and sign in with the original staff password.
- Compare the restored purchase, receipt history, balances and report.

## 5. Verification and handover

- Show the application checks and platform build matrix for the handover commit.
- Identify the designated computer, backup custodian and person responsible for confirming financial cutoffs.
- Describe deterministic financial insights and data requirements for labeled spending estimates.
