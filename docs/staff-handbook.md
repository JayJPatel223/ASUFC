# ASUFC Finance staff handbook

Use ASUFC Finance on one designated staff computer and operating-system profile. The installed app opens from the Start menu, Applications folder or desktop launcher. It keeps the ledger on that computer and works without internet access after the installer is obtained.

## 1. Open the app

Use the installer supplied by your ASUFC project lead. On Windows x64, open the installer and launch **ASUFC Finance** from Start. On macOS, open the disk image for your Mac's chip and drag the app into **Applications**. On Linux x64, open the AppImage supplied for your distribution. First launch asks for a local staff password of at least 16 characters. The login name is **staff**.

Use the installed app for day-to-day work. The GitHub Pages demo contains fictional sample records and resets when its page reloads.

## 2. Set up the 2026–27 ledger

1. In a new ledger, open **Excel import** and select the private ASUFC starter file. Check its funding rows and the editable reporting dates. Click **Apply starter file** only after reviewing them.
2. In **Funding & grants**, check supplier and purchase restrictions. Confirm expected amounts when funding is actually received. If a donation amount changes, update its award first and then record the received amount.
3. In **Programs & budgets**, assign part of a confirmed funding balance to an eligible program. The same program can have several budget lines from different sources.
4. In **Transactions**, select a row under **Pending purchases**. Check the amount, purchase date, receipt and funding source. Enter the verified total and its allocations. Add a verification note if the row is flagged or you change the amount or date. Confirm the purchase to include it in spending and reduce the assigned balances.

The starter file uses the tracker’s receipt statuses and the provided class and exam dates for Fall and Winter. Its July 1 to June 30 reporting range is provisional. Update it in **Academic years** when ASU confirms the financial cutoffs. Filters in **Reports** can include summer and between-term purchases. Review the details of each restricted purchase against its grant conditions.

## 3. Record and review purchases

Enter the purchase date and final charge in **Transactions**. Choose the program and funding budget. Use **Add allocation** to split a purchase. Check the amount shown as unassigned before confirming. **Suggest allocations** uses the entered program, purchase type and supplier to find eligible budget lines. Review every suggestion before saving.

In **History**, choose the purchase in **Purchase receipts** and attach a PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 5 MB. Use **Receipt to replace** and record a reason when uploading a replacement. The previous file remains available in history. For a wrong transaction, use **Correct a transaction** to record a linked replacement, or **Confirm void** to reverse the spending with a reason.

## 4. Report and reconcile

Choose the academic year before opening a report. In **Reports**, select a semester or enter your own date range. Use **Financial insights** for monthly cash and purchasing credit totals, program budget use, pending funding and expiry alerts. In **Reconciliation**, run the internal ledger checks and download the balance workbook. Compare bank and supplier statements separately.

## 5. Back up and recover

1. Go to **Account → Encrypted ledger backup**. Enter and confirm a backup passphrase of at least 16 characters. Use a passphrase separate from the staff password.
2. Click **Save encrypted backup**. Choose a staff-controlled location on a separate drive or approved storage device. Wait for the save to complete and check that the file ends in `.asufcbak`.
3. Keep the passphrase in your approved password store. The backup includes the ledger, pending purchases, receipts and audit history. Make a fresh backup before replacing the app or moving computers.
4. To restore, install the same or a compatible newer app on the replacement computer or a fresh operating-system profile. On the first-launch screen choose **Restore an encrypted backup**, select the file, and enter the backup passphrase.
5. Sign in using the staff password that was active when the backup was made. Check the latest purchases, receipt history, pending rows and reconciliation totals before making the recovered ledger the one staff use.

Keep the previous ledger intact until recovery is verified. Contact the ASUFC project lead if the app gives a startup error, a backup will not restore or you need to hand over staff access.
